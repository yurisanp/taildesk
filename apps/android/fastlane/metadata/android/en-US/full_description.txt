See and control your Windows and Linux computers from your phone, from anywhere, over your own Tailscale network. No TailDesk server in between, no new account and no ads.

HOW IT WORKS
• Install the official Tailscale app on your phone and sign in with the same account as your computers.
• On the computer, open TailDesk and go to Settings → Phones → Connect a phone.
• On the phone, scan the QR code. Done: your computers show up in the list.

IN A SESSION
• Touchpad: one finger moves the pointer, tap clicks, two fingers scroll and a two-finger tap is a right click.
• Direct touch: your finger is the mouse where you touch (default on tablets).
• Pinch to zoom into the computer's screen without using more bandwidth.
• Keyboard with the right accents for any keyboard layout on the computer, plus a key strip with Esc, Tab, Ctrl, Alt, arrows, F1–F12 and Ctrl+Alt+Del.
• Multiple displays: switch with ◀ ▶.
• Saves data on mobile networks and asks the computer only for your phone's screen resolution.

SECURITY AND PRIVACY
• The screen, touches and keys go straight from your phone to your computers, encrypted by Tailscale (WireGuard) and QUIC (TLS).
• Nobody gets into a computer without the person in front of it accepting (or without the access password you set).
• No telemetry, no analytics, no ads. The camera is only used to scan the QR code.
